Arrowgami Support

Stuck on something? Here are the common questions.

I bought Remove Ads on another phone. How do I get it back?

Open the level map, tap Shop, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Does Remove Ads remove every ad?

It removes the ads between levels. The "watch an ad" buttons for an extra heart or free hints stay, but they only play when you tap them.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. Deleting the app deletes your level progress, stars and hints. Remove Ads can always be restored.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

On the home screen, tap Privacy choices. You can also limit ad tracking in your phone settings.

Is every level solvable?

Yes. Every board is built so it can be cleared. If you are stuck, tap Hint to light up an arrow that can move.
